## Law of large numbers 
give me simple definition of law of large numbers

⏺ As you repeat an experiment more and more times, the average of your results gets closer and closer to the true expected value.

  Example: Flip a coin 10 times — you might get 70% heads. Flip it 10,000 times — you'll get very close to 50% heads.

  The larger the sample, the more reliable the average.
