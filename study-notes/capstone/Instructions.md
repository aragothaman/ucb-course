The UC Berkeley Professional Certificate in ML & AI (via Emeritus) capstone is ==a culminating, real-world project where you apply machine learning and AI concepts to solve a specific industry problem==. It is designed to build a market-ready GitHub portfolio. [[1](https://exec-ed.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Brochure_COE_Haas_PCMLAI-08-12-2021_FINAL.pdf), [2](https://emeritus.org/blog/b-berkeley-ai-bootcamp-curriculum/), [3](https://em-executive.berkeley.edu/professional-certificate-machine-learning-artificial-intelligence), [4](https://digitaldefynd.com/IQ/berkeley-professional-certificate-in-machine-learning-and-artificial-intelligence-review/)]

Capstone Project Phases & Instructions

The project typically follows a structured roadmap broken into several key milestones: [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRqFoy29anA)]

- **Problem Orientation & Topic Finalization:** You must identify a real-world business challenge, define the problem statement, and establish clear objectives. [[1](https://exec-ed.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Brochure_COE_Haas_PCMLAI-08-12-2021_FINAL.pdf), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRqFoy29anA)]
- **Data Identification & Preparation:** You will source an appropriate dataset, clean the data, handle missing values, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) using libraries like `pandas` and `matplotlib`, and engineer features. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2t4rGMBcX4), [2](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArtificialInteligence/comments/16qmjjn/any_thoughts_on_this_certification_from_uc/), [3](https://github.com/andrespineda/UC_Berkeley_MLAI_Capstone), [4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WIPP5AjJ0Xs), [5](https://emeritus.org/blog/b-berkeley-ai-bootcamp-curriculum/)]
- **Model Selection & Methodology Defense:** You will select, train, and evaluate multiple algorithms (e.g., Linear/Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, or Neural Networks) using Python and `scikit-learn`. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1AyNxCHmf_s&t=4), [2](https://emeritus.org/blog/b-berkeley-ai-bootcamp-curriculum/)]
- **Final Defense & Submission:** You must document your process, typically delivering a final written professional or academic report, along with your code and presentation files. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRqFoy29anA)]

Deliverables

- **GitHub Repository:** All code, Jupyter Notebooks, and documentation must be pushed to a repository.
- **Final Report:** A documented summary of your business case, data analysis, model building, and evaluation.
- **Pre-Final & Final Defense:** A presentation and defense of your reasoning, methodology, and business impact. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=daYFszyt2VE&t=663), [2](https://emeritus.org/blog/b-berkeley-ai-bootcamp-curriculum/), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rRqFoy29anA)]
